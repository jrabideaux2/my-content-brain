# Verb + noun CTA

**Definition:** Every call to action starts with an action verb and names the specific thing that will happen. Never vague, never passive, never just "Next."

**When to use:**
- All buttons and linked CTAs across all surfaces.
- Primary actions, secondary actions, recovery paths in errors.

**When NOT to use:**
- "OK" and "Cancel" — these are acceptable standard dialog closers.
- Confirmation dialogs where the action is already named in the headline.

**Optimal length:** 2 words. 3 is acceptable. 24 characters maximum (mobile-first).

**Structure:**
```
[Action verb] + [specific object or outcome]
```

**Strong examples (Intuit):**
- "Schedule a call"
- "Give feedback"
- "Run report"
- "Create project"
- "Review order"
- "Buy now & save"
- "Start free trial"
- "Update billing info"

**Weak — avoid:**
- "Next" — what happens next?
- "Continue" — where?
- "Click here" — always name the action
- "Submit" — what are they submitting?
- "Learn more" — more about what?

**Formatting rules:**
- Sentence case: "Start free trial" not "Start Free Trial"
- No emoji, no punctuation, no special formatting in button text
- No product names unless context requires it
- Don't use confirmshaming: "No, I don't want to save money" is manipulative

**Links vs. buttons:**
- Buttons: 2–3 words, max 24 characters
- Inline links: 8–55 characters (6–8 words); must be descriptive standalone
