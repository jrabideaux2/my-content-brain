# No humor in error states

**Rule:** Never use humor, playful language, or cute copy in error messages. Even well-intentioned jokes feel dismissive when someone is stuck or frustrated.

**Why:** Error states are high-stress moments. A user whose payment failed, whose data was lost, or who can't access their account is not in the mood for "Whoops! Looks like something went sideways 🙈." Humor signals the product doesn't take the problem seriously. It erodes trust at the worst possible moment.

**How to apply:**
- Strip out: "Oops," "Whoops," "Uh oh," "Yikes," "Hmm," "Ruh roh"
- Strip out: emoji in error messages
- Strip out: exclamation points in error headlines (they read as cheerful)
- Replace with: calm, direct, solution-forward language

**One exception:** Very minor, fully recoverable micro-errors (e.g., a form field typo caught in real time) may use a slightly lighter tone — but still no Oops, and still no emoji.

**Test:** Read the error message out loud as if you are the support agent. Would you say this to a frustrated customer on the phone? If not, rewrite it.
