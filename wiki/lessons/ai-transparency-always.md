# Always signal when the customer is talking to AI

**Rule:** Never let a customer believe they're talking to a human when they're talking to AI. Always make AI involvement clear at the start of an interaction and when AI generates content the customer will act on.

**Why:** Trust is the foundation of any financial product relationship. Customers discovering mid-conversation that they weren't talking to a human feel deceived — even if the experience was good. Transparency about AI involvement is also increasingly a legal and regulatory expectation.

**How to apply:**
- At the start of a bot/AI interaction: "Hi, I'm [name], Intuit's automated assistant."
- When AI generates a recommendation the customer will review: "AI generated this — review before [action]."
- Disclaimers: brief, plain language, positioned near the AI-generated content. Example: "AI can make mistakes. [Learn more]."
- Don't mystify AI: focus on what it does for the customer, not how it works technically.
- Don't oversell AI capabilities — set accurate expectations.

**What not to do:**
- Fake a human name/persona without disclosure
- Let AI-generated content appear without a review prompt in consequential contexts (tax filings, financial decisions)
- Use jargon about AI: avoid "machine learning," "neural network," "model" in user-facing copy — say what it does, not how it works
