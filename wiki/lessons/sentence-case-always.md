# Sentence case everywhere

**Rule:** All headings, button labels, nav items, and field labels use sentence case. Not Title Case. Not ALL CAPS (except specific exceptions).

**Why:** Sentence case is more conversational, more readable, saves space, and aids translation. Title Case reads as formal and brand-y — the opposite of the friendly, knowledgeable friend Intuit voice aims for.

**How to apply:**
- Headings: "Let's set up your sales tax center" not "Let's Set Up Your Sales Tax Center"
- Buttons: "Start free trial" not "Start Free Trial"
- Labels: "mileage reports" not "Mileage Reports" (unless it's a trademarked product name)
- Branded products are the exception: "QuickBooks Online," "TurboTax," "Mailchimp" always capitalized

**Allowed ALL CAPS exceptions:**
- Marketing page badges and callouts
- Navigation tabs (some contexts)
- Table headers
- "OK" (the only button that stays capitalized)

**Catch this:** If a designer delivers mockups with Title Case labels, flag it before writing to it — the visual sets expectations for copy.
